Design-01

Files:
- header.html : paste into the Nintex HTML/Text control used for the header.
- style.css   : full stylesheet containing your Nintex theme overrides plus this header design.

Important:
- Replace the logo placeholder URL(s) in header.html with your hosted GA@WORK logo URL.
- Designs 01 and 02 also require the Georgia state asset URL.
- Upload the asset files from the package's /assets folder to SharePoint or another location accessible to the form, then use that hosted URL in the HTML.
- Do not keep the old .custom-form-header CSS block when using these versions; the new ga-header classes replace it.
